Herb & Cheese Skillet Eggs
Breakfast · 10 mins · serves 4 · vegetarian

Ten minutes, one pan, and whatever herbs are still alive on your windowsill.

Ingredients
  • 8 eggs
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • 4 tbsp chopped soft herbs
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • salt and pepper
Method
  1. Melt butter in a skillet over medium-low heat.
  2. Whisk eggs with salt and pepper, pour into the skillet.
  3. Sprinkle cheese and herbs as the eggs set.
  4. Fold gently and remove while still soft. Serve at once.
